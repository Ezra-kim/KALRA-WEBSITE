About KALRA

The Kenya Agricultural and Livestock Research Agency coordinates agricultural research in Kenya. Our scientists work on crops, livestock, land and water so that improved seed, breeds, practices and advice reach people who farm for a living.

We work alongside county governments, universities, farmer groups and private companies, and we publish what we learn so others can build on it.

Mission
Generate and promote agricultural technologies, innovations and knowledge that raise productivity and livelihoods.
Vision
A food-secure Kenya supported by a strong, innovative agricultural research system.

Research programmes

Pick a programme to see what we work on.

Crops

Improved varieties of staple, horticultural and industrial crops that yield well, resist pests and disease, and cope with drought.

  • Seed and variety development
  • Pest and disease management
  • Soil fertility and good agronomy

Livestock

Better breeds, feeds and animal health practices for dairy, beef, small ruminants, poultry and bees.

  • Breeding and genetics
  • Feed and pasture improvement
  • Disease control and animal health

Land and water

Practical ways to protect soil, use water well and keep farmland productive in drylands and highlands alike.

  • Soil and water conservation
  • Irrigation and water harvesting
  • Climate-smart land management

Markets and value

Research that helps farmers store, process and sell more of what they grow, and earn more from it.

  • Post-harvest handling and storage
  • Food processing and safety
  • Market and policy analysis
